ARCHITECTURE

TF-IDF + Feed-forward ANN

Word and two-word TF-IDF features feed dense ReLU layers with dropout, followed by a sigmoid output. Training uses Adam and binary cross-entropy.

EXPLAINABILITY LIMIT

What the explanation means

The app reports terms present in the message that have high inverse-document-frequency weighting. This is a transparent input-feature summary, not a causal explanation of the neural network decision or evidence of malicious intent. Confidence is not proof that a message is safe.
